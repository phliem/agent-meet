# Consented V2 reply reminders

Reply reminders apply only to V2 account dinners. The organiser opts in at creation with `remindersEnabled: true`. A guest separately chooses the unchecked reminder checkbox when accepting their personal invitation. There is no inferred consent from attendance, an email update subscription, pilot access or an organiser's choice.

`redeemPersonalInvitation(dinnerId, invitationToken, remindersAllowed = false)` records that explicit choice on the claimed guest. The recipient can later change it through `/account?dinner=ID` or `dinnerAction(ID, { type: 'reminder-preference', allowed: false })`. Only that verified account guest can change its preference. Sign-in preserves the preference page URL. Disabling the account stops all reminders; reset clears consent and identity. V1 guests are unaffected. SMS is unavailable because verified phone contacts and an SMS transport do not exist.

A durable `reminder_jobs` record is unique to dinner, guest, reply deadline and claim generation. For windows of at least 24 hours, it is due 24 hours before the deadline. For shorter windows, it is due halfway between dinner creation and the deadline. A guest who accepts after the due time can receive the one reminder on the next worker run, provided the deadline has not passed. Extending the deadline suppresses pending jobs for the old deadline and creates a new generation at the new due time. A sent reminder is never resent for the same deadline and claim generation.

The existing authenticated `/api/jobs` endpoint runs reminders alongside subscribed dinner updates through `runJobs`. Configure its existing `CRON_SECRET`, `APP_URL`, `EMAIL_MODE=live`, `RESEND_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM`, and arrange for the existing job endpoint to be called regularly (for example every five minutes). Apply `004_reminders.sql` through `pnpm db:migrate`. The job table has RLS enabled and no browser access policies. It stores hashes and internal identity references, never a raw session token or invitation token.

The worker shares a maximum of four delivery attempts across both queues per invocation and reserves time for cleanup within the route's 60-second limit. It checks the current time again after acquiring locks and immediately before dispatch, so a slow earlier email cannot authorise a later reminder after its deadline. Retry jobs remain durable when the invocation's time budget is spent.

Before sending, the worker locks the dinner, account and job rows and checks the current claim, consent, organiser option, stage, included group, account disabled state, normalised verified account email, deadline and dinner expiry. Replies (including declines), account changes, resets, exclusions, expired dinners and confirmed dinners suppress queued delivery. Dinner deletion cascades to its jobs. Deadline changes invalidate old jobs even before they are due. Expired account sessions are removed by the same worker.

The reminder points to the dinner and the account preference page. These URLs do not grant access: the recipient must sign in with their invited verified email. A claimed guest does not receive a fresh single-use invitation token. This preserves the original single-use invitation while giving the recipient their own authenticated access on another device. The preference page lets the recipient stop reminders without sharing any bearer credential.

Email uses the existing `EmailSender` interface and Resend transport. A stable provider idempotency key plus a locked durable job prevents concurrent/repeated runs from delivering duplicates. Delivery failures retry with exponential backoff, up to six attempts, then remain failed. A provider may accept a request whose network response is lost; retry uses the same idempotency key. Real delivery guarantees still depend on the provider's idempotency window. Local fixture sends retain `fixture` status and do not claim real delivery.

Verification uses a controlled clock and fake sender. The isolated local Postgres integration test covers concurrent duplicate workers, repeated runs, retries, deadline extension, opt-out, disabled accounts, confirmation, reset, declined reply, excluded guests, changed verified contacts and dinner deletion. No production email or SMS has been sent. Provider delivery and inbox appearance remain human-verification work once live email credentials exist.
